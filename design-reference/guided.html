Conflict

Conflict is the problem that drives the plot of a story. Without it, nothing would happen. There are 4 main types of conflict.

CHARACTER VS. SELF

A character struggles with their own feelings or a hard choice.

CHARACTER VS. CHARACTER

Two characters want different things or are against each other.

CHARACTER VS. NATURE

A character faces a force of nature they cannot control, like a storm.

CHARACTER VS. SOCIETY

A character is treated unfairly or struggles against rules or money.
